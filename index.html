Everything the school sent, in one week

Due This Week turns the PDFs and emails piling up in your inbox into one checklist for the week — across every kid in the house.

Share a letter in from Mail, Files or Photos. Due This Week reads it, shows you what it found, and you tick what is real. By Sunday evening you know the five things the week needs and which of you is doing each.

  • Share a letter in, get a checklist out
  • Every document read on your phone. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.
  • One list across every child in the house
  • A nudge on Sunday, and again the day before

It replaces the re-typing, not the school's portal. ParentSquare, Canvas, Librus and the rest are one school, one child, one login, and they end at "here is your notice". What has no home is the bit underneath: three PDFs, two kids, one Saturday, and who is buying the kit.

What it costs

Free to download. Onboarding is free. After that it is $1.49 a week or $6.99 once — a little over four weeks of the subscription buys it outright, so buying it once is usually the honest answer.

Nothing leaves your phone

There is no account, no password and no server of ours. Documents are read using software built into iOS, on the device, and nothing read out of them is sent anywhere. The privacy policy is short because there is very little to say.
